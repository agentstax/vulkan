---
title: "lease reclaimed from expired worker"
slug: errors/VK0026
---

**Code** `VK0026` · **Log event** at warn -- degraded but self-healing, or a durable data consequence; an operator should learn of it eventually.

A consumer stopped renewing a range lease and it expired; the range
returned to the claimable pool and another consumer redelivers its
messages. Attempt counts survive the reclaim. Frequent reclaims point at
consumers dying mid-batch or processing that outruns the lease's margins.

---
title: "partition_count"
slug: errors/VK0094
kind: "alert"
consequence: "warn, owned by a topic; resolves once the count is back under the threshold"
---

A topic's message log holds enough partitions that dropping the topic
approaches the lock-table ceiling.

The hourly check counts every topic's message-log partitions and publishes
an active alert for each topic at or above the job's threshold. Dropping or
destroying the topic locks about 5 relations per partition in one
transaction, and past `max_locks_per_transaction` Postgres refuses it with
`out of shared memory`. The alert's `data` carries `partition_count`,
`lock_ceiling`, and `threshold`.

Lower the topic's retention so the janitor drops old partitions, or raise
`max_locks_per_transaction`. The next run that finds the count under the
threshold publishes the resolution under the same key.

```
vulkan alert list
```

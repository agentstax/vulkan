---
title: "abandoned-routine events dropped"
slug: errors/VK0052
kind: "event"
level: "warn"
consequence: "degraded but self-healing, or a durable data consequence; an operator should learn of it eventually"
---

A consumer instance discarded abandoned/cleared routine events instead of
producing them to `__system.metrics`; `dropped_count` carries how many.
Two causes share the line:

- The batch could not land -- the flush tick's produce failed, and the
  batch is dropped, not requeued. The `error` attribute carries the cause,
  usually a database problem other lines will also be reporting.
- The in-memory queue hit its cap between flush ticks (no `error` attribute).
  The cap exists so a storm of abandoned routines cannot pile up
  unbounded memory or database load; sustained cap drops mean the
  consumerFunc is hanging past its timeout at a high rate -- investigate
  the consumer ([Consumer Timeouts](/guides/consumer-timeouts/)), not
  the metrics.

The loss is permanent for those events: abandoned-routine snapshots
(`Total`, `Outstanding`, self-clear latency) undercount for the window,
and the abandoned-routine alert can miss a fire or a resolve. The
session's `abandoned_count` on the stopped line is unaffected -- it is
counted in memory, not read back from the topic.

---
title: "vulkan.schedule.state.overdue"
slug: errors/VK0070
kind: "metric"
consequence: "gauge of `{found}`, one series system-wide"
---

Unsuspended schedules whose next production time is past the overdue
threshold.

The value is the count found during the collector's live schedule read. A
suspended schedule never enters it, no matter how old its next scheduled time
is.

---
title: "produce exceeded the duration threshold"
slug: errors/VK0038
kind: "event"
level: "warn"
consequence: "degraded but self-healing, or a durable data consequence; an operator should learn of it eventually"
---

One produce call ran longer than the producer's `SlowProduceThreshold`.
The line carries the call's `duration` beside the `threshold`; the call's
outcome is unaffected and slowness is reported whether the call succeeded
or not. `ProduceFunc` and `ProduceInTx` durations include the caller's
own closure and transaction time.

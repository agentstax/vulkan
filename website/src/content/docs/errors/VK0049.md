**Code** `VK0049` · **Metric** -- counter of `{range}`, one series per consumer session.

Ranges that hit the reclaim cap and were written out as independent
`'ready'` exceptions instead of being handed out again.

A session flow: this instance's own running total, flushed to
`__system.metrics` each flush tick and printed as `quarantined_count` on
the [consumer stopped](/errors/VK0041) line. It never reconciles against
the fleet-wide `vulkan.consumer.*` gauges.

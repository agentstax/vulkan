---
title: "delivery cannot succeed"
slug: errors/VK0055
kind: "error"
recovery: "permanent"
consequence: "the delivery dead-letters on this attempt; no retry runs"
---

`vulkan.Terminal(cause)` returned from a consumerFunc. The
delivery's row goes to `dead` at once -- whatever the retry budget had
left -- with this text and the wrapped cause as `last_error`, and the
delivery_log row for this attempt carries status `failure`.

Any error carrying a Permanent classification classifies the same way;
`Terminal` is the spelling for a cause that has none of its own.
[Handler outcomes](/guides/handler-outcomes/) has the whole table.

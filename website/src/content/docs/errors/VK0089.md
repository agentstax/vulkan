title: "vulkan.consumer.exceptions.oldest_unresolved_age"

kind: "metric"
consequence: "gauge of `ms`, one series per consumer group"
---

The age of the oldest ready, inflight, or deferred delivery row.

The `topic` and `group` attributes identify the series. Zero means no
unresolved exception row exists. Dead rows do not take part because they no
longer block progress.

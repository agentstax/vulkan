---
title: "compaction_read_cost"
slug: errors/VK0095
kind: "alert"
consequence: "warn, owned by a topic; resolves once the count is back under the threshold"
---

A compacted topic holds enough partitions that replaying a never-superseded
key is a long scan.

A consumer replaying a key nothing has superseded scans from that key's
partition to the current tail. The cost grows with the partition count,
about 10µs per partition, and never amortizes. The hourly check publishes
an active alert for each compacted topic at or above the job's threshold;
the alert's `data` carries `partition_count` and `threshold`.

Compact more aggressively or lower retention so old partitions drop, which
bounds the replay. The next run that finds the count under the threshold
publishes the resolution under the same key.

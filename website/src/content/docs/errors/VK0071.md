title: "vulkan.schedule.state.oldest_due_age"

consequence: "gauge of `ms`, one series system-wide"
---

The longest time any unsuspended schedule is past its next production time.

Zero means no unsuspended schedule is currently due. The value starts growing
as soon as a schedule is due; the overdue count uses its separate configured
threshold.

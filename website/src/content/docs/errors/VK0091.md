---
title: "vulkan.consumer.abandoned_routines.outstanding"
slug: errors/VK0091
kind: "metric"
consequence: "gauge of `{routine}`, one series per consumer group"
---

Abandoned consumerFunc routines with no matching cleared record.

The `topic` and `group` attributes identify the series. A routine enters this
count after the hard timeout writes it off and leaves when that same routine
eventually returns and records its clear.

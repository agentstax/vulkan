Every design that settles gets a numbered record: the context, the
decision, and its consequences, written in the session the design
settled and never edited afterward. A decision that gets replaced keeps
its record — the old one flips to superseded and the two link both
ways, so the trail stays whole. Records read as they were written:
their vocabulary is the codebase's at the time, and citations such as
`[0590]` name the record they came from.

Numbers were allocated in per-phase blocks when the older history was
distilled, so gaps are normal. Newest first.
